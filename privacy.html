Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Prime Beta Games ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games for Android and iOS devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our games, website, and related services ("Services").

We handle information according to our actual practices and applicable privacy laws. Where consent or other choices are required by law, we provide those choices as applicable.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you contact us, you may provide your email address, support request, feedback, bug report, or other information you choose to submit.

Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the particular game, its features, permissions, and integrated services, information may include device information, operating system version, language, approximate location, IP address, advertising identifiers, crash reports, gameplay statistics, session duration, purchase-related information, and advertising interactions.

Not every game collects every category of information. Information collected depends on the features and technologies used by that game.

2. How We Use Your Information

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain our games and website.
  • Save or manage game progress where supported.
  • Improve gameplay and user experience.
  • Identify and fix bugs or crashes.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Support purchases where available.
  • Display and measure advertisements where integrated.
  • Help prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Advertising

Some of our games may display advertisements. If an advertising service is integrated into a game, that service may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, or ad interactions to deliver ads, control ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure performance.

Depending on the game, your location, and applicable requirements, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Consent and privacy choices are provided where required.

4. In-App Purchases

Some games may offer optional in-app purchases. Where purchases are available, they are generally processed by the relevant platform provider, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment information is handled according to the platform provider's processes and policies.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on the game, we may use third-party services for app distribution, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, messaging, attribution, or payment processing. Providers and services can differ between games and releases.

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Specific providers should be disclosed according to the services actually used in the relevant game.

6. Data Sharing

We may share relevant information with service providers when necessary to operate our services, provide support, display or measure advertisements, analyze performance, process purchases, prevent fraud, maintain security, or comply with applicable law.

Our data-sharing practices depend on the services integrated into each game and applicable legal requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games may have different intended audiences. We aim to configure each game and its services for its intended age group and comply with applicable children's privacy requirements. Where required, we apply appropriate data-collection limits, advertising practices, and parental-consent processes.

Parents or guardians with privacy questions or concerns may contact us using the details provided below.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational needs, dispute resolution, and applicable legal obligations.

10. International Data Transfers

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also be able to withdraw consent or manage advertising choices through your device settings or in-game privacy tools.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us at support@primebetagames.site.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies where applicable to support website functionality, understand traffic, maintain security, or provide other disclosed services. Available cookie controls depend on the technologies used.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our games, or our services, please contact us.
