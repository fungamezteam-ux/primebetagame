Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Prime Beta Games ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games for Android and iOS devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our games, website, and related services ("Services").

By downloading or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy, subject to any consent or choices required by applicable law.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Customer support requests
  • Email address when contacting support
  • Feedback and bug reports
  • Information submitted voluntarily

Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the game, features, permissions, and third-party services used, information may include:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Language and region
  • Country and approximate location
  • IP address
  • Advertising Identifier (AAID / IDFA where applicable and permitted)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Game progress and gameplay statistics
  • Session duration
  • Purchase status or history relevant to the game
  • Ad interaction information
  • Fraud prevention signals

2. How We Use Your Information

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain our games
  • Save game progress
  • Improve gameplay experience
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Analyze player behavior and game performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Process or validate in-app purchases
  • Display personalized or contextual advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Advertising

Our games may be offered free of charge and may display advertisements. Advertising revenue can help support game development and updates. Depending on the game and the advertising partners integrated, those partners may collect or receive device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising identifiers, and usage or ad-interaction information to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure campaign performance.

Depending on your region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized, and consent or privacy choices may be presented where required by law.

4. In-App Purchases

Some games may offer optional in-app purchases, such as virtual items, ad removal, premium content, or game currency. Purchases are processed by the relevant platform provider, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details through these platform purchases.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on the game, we may integrate third-party SDKs or services for app distribution, advertising, analytics, cloud services, crash reporting, messaging, attribution, and payment processing. These may include, where actually integrated, Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Analytics, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), AppLovin MAX, AppLovin Exchange, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, ironSource, Liftoff Monetize, Pangle, Chartboost, InMobi, Amazon Publisher Services, Tenjin, Adjust, AppsFlyer, and Apple App Store Services.

The services used can differ between games and releases. Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review the policies of providers used by the particular game you play.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with relevant service providers when reasonably necessary to display advertisements, measure ad performance, provide analytics, detect fraud, process purchases, deliver cloud services, improve game performance, or comply with applicable laws.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games may have different intended audiences. We aim to configure each game and its services for its intended age group and comply with applicable children's privacy requirements, including Google Play Families requirements, COPPA, and GDPR-K where applicable. Where required, we limit data collection, use age-appropriate advertising practices, and seek consent from a parent or guardian. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions or concerns.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our Services, meet legitimate operational needs, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete certain personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or request data portability. You may also be able to manage advertising and privacy choices through your device settings or in-game consent tools.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and certain third-party services may use cookies, SDK technologies, and similar identifiers to support functionality, analyze traffic, prevent abuse, and personalize experiences where permitted.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our Services or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.
